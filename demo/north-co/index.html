LESS INVENTORY.
MORE DISCERNMENT.

North & Co. is a fictional premium real estate brand concept created to demonstrate how a property business can combine editorial storytelling, modern digital design, and conversion-focused user experience.

The philosophy is simple: fewer properties, presented better. Deeper attention to architecture, context, and the way a space supports the life that happens inside it.
