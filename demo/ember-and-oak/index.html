04 — Our Story

BUILT AROUND FIRE.

EMBER & OAK began with a simple obsession: what happens when exceptional ingredients meet real fire?

We built a kitchen around open flame, a dining room around intimacy, and a menu around the ingredients that deserve both.

This is not a restaurant about noise. It's about heat, patience, and the quiet moment before the first bite.
